01 — About

A Class 10 student who would rather build than just read about it.

I’m Asif Shahrukh Khan from Kuttippuram, Thazhava, Karunagappally, Kollam — studying in Class 10 at SMHSS Patharam. I’m an IT enthusiast, developer, hardware builder, PC enthusiast and technology explorer, doing all of this alongside school.

The building started early. As a kid I was already taking RC cars apart, modifying them and making my own. That same itch now lives in ESP32 boards, websites, GPUs and full devices. I learn by making things real: wiring a board, shipping a dashboard, debugging the noise on a speaker.

I’m also a huge AI enthusiast. I explore ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Qwen, DeepSeek, NVIDIA Nemotron and other models — comparing how they handle real coding tasks in Arduino C++, ESP32 firmware, debugging and multi-file web projects. AI is a development partner, not an autopilot: I test, modify and debug what it gives me.

I would describe myself as a tech guy who likes experimenting.

05 — The rig

A real desk, not a render.

The everyday machine I use to code, learn, experiment and game — with lighting I built around it.

Built to feel like a workspace.

A compact black desk, a black tower, a monitor and a lot of light. I hand-made foam wall art — a Batman-style emblem, an ROG-inspired badge and a Spider-Man symbol — and placed RGB lighting behind the screen, under the desk and around the tower.

The lighting is the interesting part: an ESP32 running WLED, ambient effects, screen-colour and audio-reactive experiments, and ideas about showing PC stats as light. Some of those are still works in progress.
