01 — About

A 15-year-old who would rather build than just read about it.

I’m Asif Shahrukh Khan from Kuttippuram, Thazhava, Karunagappally, Kollam — studying in Class 10 at SMHSS Patharam. I’m an IT enthusiast, developer, hardware builder, PC enthusiast and technology explorer.

The building started early. As a kid I was already taking RC cars apart, modifying them and making my own. That same itch now lives in ESP32 boards, websites, GPUs and full devices. I learn by making things real: wiring a board, shipping a dashboard, debugging the noise on a speaker.

My biggest obsession is ESP32 and embedded electronics — displays, buttons, audio, SD cards, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, batteries, charging circuits, amplifiers, custom hardware. Alongside that I build modern full-stack websites, often with AI as a development partner.

I would describe myself as a tech guy who likes experimenting.

Embedded · Gaming

ESP32 NES Game Console

A compact handheld built around an ESP32: ILI9341 TFT, physical buttons, NES emulator firmware, audio hardware, SD-card experiments, TP4056 charging, voltage booster and custom wiring. Not “install an emulator” — a complete DIY embedded gaming device. Hardware, mapping, power, audio, firmware and the long troubleshooting trail in between.

  • Emulator
  • TP4056
